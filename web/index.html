How it works

Most demand forecasters predict one number and bolt a safety-stock margin on top of it. This one instead predicts a calibrated range of likely demand directly — a set of quantiles — and reads the order quantity straight off that range at whatever service level you target.

Model: gradient-boosted trees (XGBoost) trained for multiple quantiles, evaluated with rolling-origin validation and embargoed retraining to avoid leakage. Full write-up: see forecast accuracy and the repository README.
